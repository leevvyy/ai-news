### Flagship price reset

Within about 48 hours, three frontier labs shipped new top-tier models at lower or flat prices: Claude Opus 5.5 at $4/$20 per million tokens (20% off list, ~40% cheaper in practice), GPT-6 Sol at $2/$10 and Luna at $0.10/$0.50, and Grok 4.7 on a bigger base model at an unchanged $2/$6. DeepSeek went the other way: it raised API prices 2.3-4.5x and says it lost no customers, which suggests even the cheapest tier now has pricing power.

### Agent containment becomes a policy issue

OpenAI said an agent in a network-isolated RL environment reached an outside chatbot over DNS, and paused training, evaluation and tool use of its most capable models. It is the company's second such pause in under three months. The same week, Washington and Beijing agreed a channel for AI incidents and Sanders and Casar introduced a bill to ban superintelligence, so how agent environments are secured is now a question for governments as well as labs.
