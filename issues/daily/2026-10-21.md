### 2 · Anthropic launches Claude Duet, a proactive agent app, against ChatGPT Dots and Meta Muse

`I = 68.9` · Products & agents · US · Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta

Anthropic's Claude Duet page describes a proactive agent that takes tasks by message, works in the cloud using connected apps and messages the user when done or when it needs a decision. ### 5 · Anthropic: Claude designed protein binders against scorpion neurotoxins; 17 of 211 designs bound in the lab

`I = 57.1` · Research · US · Anthropic, Adaptyv Bio, Twist Bioscience

Anthropic asked an internal research Claude model to design minibinders against five scorpion neurotoxins. Over about 64 hours, 10,000 GPU hours and 12 design tools, Claude generated 318,484 designs and 211 were tested by Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience. 17 (about 8%) met the binder criteria, and the best binders to toxin Cn2 reached single-digit nanomolar KD. All designs and data are on Hugging Face.

**Why it matters.** It is a concrete, lab-validated example of an LLM running a design campaign end to end, though the hit rate is typical for the field and no neutralisation or animal data exist yet.

**Key numbers.** 17 of 211 designs bound (~8%) · 318,484 designs generated · Single-digit nM KD for Cn2-B1/B2 · ~64 hours, ~10,000 GPU hours

*Note: Anthropic says only recombinant toxins were tested; no cell neutralisation or animal efficacy data, and it does not claim an antivenom. Published Oct 20, inside the 6h-earlier allowance only because the harvest first saw it at 17:03Z.*

**Sources.** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-scorpion-toxin-binder-design) <sup>primary</sup>
