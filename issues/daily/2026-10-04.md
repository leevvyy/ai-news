### 1 · Trump names DNI Jay Clayton to lead 'Super Intelligence Force' AI task force with 120-day report deadline

`I = 73.8` · Policy & safety · US · White House, Jay Clayton, Andrew Ferguson

President Trump announced a White House AI task force, branded the Super Intelligence Force, led by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton as AI czar. ### 2 · Broadcom to lend Anthropic up to $42B for TPU leases as banks syndicate $60B debt package

`I = 65.6` · Business & funding · US · Broadcom, Anthropic, Blackstone · `AVGO`

Anthropic's IPO filing, as reported by Reuters on Oct 1, shows Broadcom agreeing to lend up to $42B to fund Anthropic's chip leases, with notes that can convert into Anthropic equity. That covers roughly one-third of Anthropic's $125.2B five-year commitment to lease Google-designed TPU capacity that Broadcom helps build. Reports on Oct 2 say Bank of America, Citi and Morgan Stanley are sending syndication letters for a $42B senior-secured tranche, with Blackstone leading an $18B junior tranche. A 24/7 Wall St. piece on Oct 4 frames it as circular financing.

**Why it matters.** Broadcom becomes supplier, lessor, lender and potential shareholder to one customer, the clearest sign yet that AI compute is being financed through vendor credit and structured debt.

**Key numbers.** $42B Broadcom loan · $125.2B five-year TPU lease commitment · $60B total debt package · $18B Blackstone junior tranche

### 3 · Amazon explores $8B off-balance-sheet SPV for Nvidia Grace Blackwell chips, FT reports

`I = 59.6` · Compute & chips · US · Amazon, Nvidia · `AMZN` `NVDA`

Amazon is discussing selling about $8B of Nvidia Grace Blackwell chips into a special-purpose vehicle and leasing them back, per the Financial Times. The SPV would issue debt, targeting an investment-grade rating tied to Amazon's AA credit, and Amazon may offer outside investors up to a 10% equity stake. The chips sit across more than a dozen US data centers in five states. Talks are ongoing and Amazon declined to comment; Amazon expects about $220B of capex this year.

**Why it matters.** A hyperscaler moving GPUs off its books shows capex financing shifting to structured credit, and adds to the Broadcom-Anthropic debate over how sustainable AI buildout funding is.

**Key numbers.** $8B of Nvidia chips · up to 10% equity offered · ~$220B Amazon 2026 capex · 5 US states

### 4 · Microsoft and Hugging Face release ThinkingBox, grading agents on database state across 507 workflows

`I = 58.7` · Research · US · Microsoft, Hugging Face · `MSFT`

ThinkingBox is a sandbox and benchmark of 507 stateful business workflows (retail, travel, insurance), each run 20 times and graded on terminal backend state rather than the agent's final message. Microsoft's write-up says 67.24% of failed attempts still ended cleanly, called a state-changing tool and reported no error. Secondary coverage reports Claude Opus 5.5 leading at 67.16% pass@1 versus 58.31% for GPT-6 Astra, with Opus 5.5 passing all 20 runs on 241 tasks.

**Why it matters.** It quantifies the gap between an agent saying it is done and the data actually being right, and pushes evals toward repeated-run reliability instead of single-attempt success.

**Key numbers.** 507 tasks · 20 runs per task · 67.24% of failures exit cleanly · Opus 5.5 67.16% pass@1 · GPT-6 Astra 58.31% pass@1 · 241 of 507 tasks passed 20/20 (Opus 5.5)
