### 1 · Google Cloud launches 'Gemini agent', a universal enterprise work agent that can route to Claude

`I = 69.9` · Products & agents · US · Google Cloud, Google · `GOOGL`

At Gemini at Work 2026, Google Cloud unveiled the Gemini agent, a single agent for knowledge work, media creation and coding that works across Gemini Enterprise, Workspace, Slack, Microsoft 365, the command line and mobile. It picks a model per task, starting with Gemini and Claude, and can spawn temporary job-specific sub-agents; teams can also create 'coworker' agents with their own mailbox, calendar and Drive. An API exposes it as a headless agent. It is in private preview, with wide availability for select Workspace Business and Enterprise plans promised 'soon'.

**Why it matters.** Google is consolidating its enterprise agent products into one surface and openly orchestrating a rival's model, raising the stakes against OpenAI's always-on dots agents and Microsoft's Copilot.

**Key numbers.** Private preview · Models: Gemini + Claude at launch

### 2 · Samsung guides Q3 operating profit to a record 107.4 trillion won on ~195T won sales

`I = 68.6` · Compute & chips · Other · Samsung Electronics, SK hynix · `005930.KS`

Samsung Electronics' preliminary Q3 2026 results put operating profit at about 107.4 trillion won (~$80B), its first quarter above 100 trillion won, on sales of about 195 trillion won. Operating profit rose roughly 20% from Q2 and 782% year on year, a 55.1% margin, and beat a consensus near 106 trillion won while sales came in slightly below the ~199T consensus. The figures are preliminary; the full division breakdown is due Oct 29. Seoul shares slipped on the day and the KOSPI fell, while SK hynix rose.

**Why it matters.** It confirms the memory upcycle is still accelerating through Q3 and sets the bar for SK hynix and Micron prints later this month; the muted share reaction suggests the good news was already priced in.

### 3 · Manus raises over $500M, its first round since Meta's $2B acquisition was unwound

### 5 · South Korea orders probe as AI is suspected in hacks of seven banks exposing about 68,000 people

`I = 58.8` · Policy & safety · Other · South Korea, Shinhan Bank, KB Kookmin Bank, Hana Bank

> 原标题：韩国多家银行被黑，疑似有人工智能参与，李在明要求彻查

President Lee Jae-myung told an Oct 6 cabinet meeting there are signs AI was used in recent attacks on Korean banks and ordered a thorough investigation. **Why it matters.** It is a concrete government-level acknowledgement of AI-assisted attacks on financial infrastructure, adding to the agent-misuse enforcement wave.
