### 1 · Google launches Gemini 4 Carbon, topping Artificial Analysis Index v5 and pitched at cybersecurity and long-horizon agents

`I = 76.4` · Models & labs · US · Google, Google DeepMind, Gemini 4 Carbon

Two weeks after Gemini 4 Argon, Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon as its most capable model, with 'significant gains' in coding, agentic tasks and cybersecurity. Google says it ranks first on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 (score 59, per IT之家), ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra (The Verge). IT之家 reports introductory API pricing of $2 per million input and $10 per million output tokens, rising to $4/$20 after the introductory period, with a 95% discount on cached input, and a 1,769 score (4th) on WebDev Arena, up 89 Elo. Ars Technica notes that, like Argon, Carbon is on a staged release that could take a while.

**Why it matters.** A new top-of-index frontier model at the same entry price as Argon pressures OpenAI and Anthropic on price-performance, and Google is leaning on cyber-defence capability as the differentiator. Access is staged, so developers may not be able to use it yet.

**Key numbers.** AA Intelligence Index v5.0: 59, #1 · $2 / $10 per M tokens intro; $4 / $20 after · 95% cached-input discount · WebDev Arena 1,769 (#4), +89 Elo

*Note: Google's own pages (DeepMind blog) were not opened; figures come from feed summaries of The Verge, Ars Technica (partial) and IT之家 (partial; pricing, 59 score and Arena Elo are IT之家's). Availability details beyond 'staged release' were not seen.*
