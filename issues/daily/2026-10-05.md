### 1 · Reflection AI unveils Beam, a 501B-parameter open-weight MoE; Apache 2.0 weights promised later in October

`I = 75.9` · Models & labs · US · Reflection AI, Z.ai, Moonshot AI, Nvidia

Nvidia-backed Reflection debuted its first model, Beam, on Oct 5: a 501B-total, 23B-active MoE pretrained on 23.8T tokens with a 1M-token context. Reflection says it is competitive with Z.ai's GLM-5.2 on coding and agentic tasks while using 3-4x less inference compute. Early access is by waitlist; weights, a technical report and a model card are due under Apache 2.0 later in October. TechCrunch notes the claims are unverified, and Beam trails Kimi K3 on Terminal-Bench and DeepSWE.

**Why it matters.** It is the first serious US-built open-weight challenger to the Chinese models that dominate open rankings. The weights have not shipped, so independent testing is still pending.

**Key numbers.** 501B total / 23B active params · 23.8T pretraining tokens · 1M context · Terminal-Bench v2.1: 80.1 (Kimi K3 88.3) · SWE-Bench Verified: 80.9 (vendor-reported) · Valuation $25B

*Note: Facts from search snippets only; no page was opened. Benchmark scores are vendor-reported and unverified per TechCrunch. Publish time approximate. No price published; weights not yet released.*

### 2 · Huawei and Qualcomm sign multi-year patent deal covering 5G and AI; Qualcomm to buy Huawei US patents

`I = 71.8` · Business & funding · China · Huawei, Qualcomm · `QCOM`

> 原标题：华为与高通达成多年专利许可协议，高通将收购华为部分美国专利

On Oct 5 Huawei and Qualcomm announced a multi-year cross-licence across 5G, computing, AI and networking. Qualcomm will also buy selected Huawei US patents in compute, AI and networking. Reports say Qualcomm becomes the net payer for the first time in the firms' 25-year licensing history, and this is the first Huawei-Qualcomm deal to cover 5G. The deal needs regulatory approval; financial terms and timing were not disclosed.

**Why it matters.** A US chip leader paying a sanctioned Chinese rival for AI and compute patents reverses the usual IP flow and signals the commercial value of Huawei's R&D.

**Key numbers.** Multi-year deal · Qualcomm net payer for the first time in 25 years · Terms undisclosed

### 4 · NYC Council grills OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta on AI risk as ex-Anthropic researcher Coxon testifies

`I = 68.3` · Policy & safety · US · New York City Council, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google

All 51 NYC Council members questioned policy executives from Anthropic (Logan Graham), OpenAI (Morgan Dwyer), Google (Alice Friend) and Meta (Shane Cahill) under oath on Oct 5. Former Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon told the panel humanity is more likely than not to lose control of AI. Speaker Julie Menin called answers on legal liability 'troubling at best'. The firms reportedly agreed to appear only after subpoena threats. Menin's bill would bar selling or deploying an AI model in NYC unless an outside validator has reviewed it and a person can shut it down.

**Why it matters.** A US city is moving toward binding frontier-AI safeguards while federal policy stays light-touch, adding to the state and local patchwork the labs face.

**Key numbers.** 51 council members · 4 AI companies testifying · Bill: outside validator plus human shutdown

### 5 · Pentagon tells BBC it has ceased using Anthropic products, though sources say Claude was still used via Maven last week

`I = 65.7` · Policy & safety · US · Anthropic, US Department of Defense, Palantir
