### 1 · Eight US states sue OpenAI, alleging ChatGPT harms children and was marketed as safe

`I = 77.9` · Policy & safety · US · OpenAI

Attorneys general from eight states sued OpenAI on Oct 14 over ChatGPT's safety for minors. Iowa, Kansas, Nebraska and West Virginia announced suits at a joint press conference, while Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho and Montana filed separately in their own state courts. Iowa's suit invokes its Consumer Fraud Act and alleges OpenAI falsely portrayed ChatGPT as safe for ages 13 and up. The states seek court orders changing how ChatGPT treats young users, plus civil penalties and damages.

**Why it matters.** It is the first multi-state litigation wave against a frontier lab on child safety, following Florida's lone suit in June. It raises OpenAI's legal and product-compliance exposure on age gating and youth features.

**Key numbers.** 8 states · 4 joint filers (IA, KS, NE, WV) · 4 separate filers (AR, AZ, ID, MT)

### 2 · FBI arrests OpenAI engineer charged with planting hidden recording device in executive conference room

`I = 74.7` · Policy & safety · US · OpenAI

The Justice Department charged Saudi national Abdulrahman Alfozan after OpenAI staff found a roughly $14 ESP32-based device hidden in toilet paper behind a TV in a San Francisco executive conference room, installed around Sept 28. Prosecutors say a ChatGPT account tied to his spouse was used for prompts on building and hiding such a device, and Bloomberg reports a court filing shows a one-way ticket to Saudi Arabia booked for Oct 12. OpenAI fired him and said it saw no sign of access to model weights, source code or customer data.

**Why it matters.** A physical-security and insider-threat incident at the most prominent US lab, amid scrutiny of AI-lab security. The complaint does not allege trade-secret theft or acting for a foreign government.

**Key numbers.** $14 device · Device installed ~Sept 28 · One-way ticket for Oct 12 · Detention hearing Oct 19

### 3 · Doubao phone assistant opens screen automation after 30-day notice; 151 apps, incl. 65 Tencent and 23 Alibaba, opt out

`I = 67.3` · Products & agents · China · ByteDance, Tencent, Alibaba, DeepSeek · `0700.HK` `9988.HK`

> 原标题：豆包手机助手宣布陆续放开第三方应用屏幕自动化操作权限，151款应用明确拒绝

ByteDance's Doubao phone assistant said the 30-day public-notice period for its Screen Automation Execution Protocol ended on Oct 14 and it will gradually open screen-automation permissions for third-party apps. Apps that declared a refusal will not be operated by the agent, and the platform acts within 3 working days of a refusal. The latest list shows 151 refusing apps, including WeChat, Taobao, Alipay, Meituan, Kuaishou, DeepSeek and Kimi. Media say the block only stops the AI's GUI recognition and simulated taps; the apps still work manually.

**Why it matters.** The largest Chinese super-apps are refusing to let a rival's phone agent drive their interfaces, showing how hard open agent-to-app access is without formal APIs.

**Key numbers.** 151 apps refused · 65 Tencent-ecosystem apps · 23 Alibaba-ecosystem apps · 30-day notice period

*Note: Count and Tencent/Alibaba split come from media relaying the Doubao assistant's official account; official list not seen. One outlet reports 155 apps. published_at is harvest first-seen time.*

### 4 · ASML Q3 profit beats, raises 2026 sales floor to EUR 44B; customers float orders for 2029-2030

`I = 65.7` · Compute & chips · Europe · ASML · `ASML`

ASML reported Q3 net sales of EUR 11.66B and net income of about EUR 4.96B (versus 4.1B expected), guided Q4 sales of EUR 14.2-15.2B and lifted the low end of its 2026 range to EUR 44-45B. CEO Christophe Fouquet said some customers are asking about orders for 2029 and 2030 and the company is looking at expanding capacity beyond its earlier 30% increase for 2028. SCMP reports China sales rose 109% quarter on quarter, to 23% of machine sales from 14%.

**Why it matters.** The strongest read-through yet that AI capex still pulls through lithography demand despite a chip-stock rout, and a sign of Chinese stockpiling ahead of possible tighter DUV export rules.

**Key numbers.** Q3 sales EUR 11.66B · Net income ~EUR 4.96B vs 4.1B est · Q4 guide EUR 14.2-15.2B · FY26 EUR 44-45B · China +109% QoQ
