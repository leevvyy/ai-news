### 1 · Anthropic tells investors Q3 revenue hit $16.8B, adjusted profitable, as IPO nears

`I = 76.5` · Business & funding · US · Anthropic

Anthropic reportedly told prospective investors that third-quarter revenue reached $16.8 billion, up from $11.6 billion in Q2 and about $4.7 billion in Q1, and that it was profitable on an adjusted basis. Bloomberg cites an adjusted gross margin of 85% and notes the quarter's growth was under 50% from June, a marked deceleration. **Key numbers.** Q3 revenue $16.8B · Q2 revenue $11.6B · Q1 revenue ~$4.7B · adjusted gross margin 85% · IPO valuation up to $2T

### 2 · Meta releases Muse Spark Open, an 816B-parameter open-weight model, and runs it on Macs with Apple

`I = 75.5` · Models & labs · US · Meta, Apple, Amazon Web Services, NVIDIA · `META` `AAPL`

Meta released Muse Spark Open, an open-weight multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 816B total and 42B active parameters and a 1M-token context window, aimed at coding and agentic work. Weights are on Hugging Face, Amazon SageMaker, Google Cloud Model Garden and Microsoft Foundry. Meta and Apple said it can run locally on two 512GB M5 Ultra Mac Studios using MLX, and NVIDIA published a guide to serving it on GB300 NVL72.

**Why it matters.** It is Meta's first open-weight Muse model at frontier scale and is pitched as the strongest US open-weight model, pressuring Chinese open-weight leaders. The Apple tie-up makes local use of a model this size plausible.

**Key numbers.** 816B total / 42B active parameters · 1M-token context · 2x 512GB M5 Ultra Mac Studio via MLX (per MacRumors)

### 3 · FTC sends civil subpoenas to Anthropic and OpenAI in AI safety-incident probe

`I = 74.0` · Policy & safety · US · FTC, Anthropic, OpenAI, METR

The FTC sent civil investigative demands to Anthropic and OpenAI on Oct 14, an agency spokesperson confirmed to The Hill. Axios, citing a person familiar, says each runs 50+ pages covering security incidents, safeguarding practices, marketing materials and privacy policies. The probe, first reported Sept 30 as industry-wide and including evaluator METR, is expected to extend to executive testimony and third-party AI organizations.

**Why it matters.** It moves the first federal consumer-protection probe of frontier-lab safety from reported inquiry to compulsory process, days before Anthropic's planned IPO marketing.

**Key numbers.** demands sent Oct 14 · 50+ pages each (Axios, anonymous source) · probe first reported Sept 30

### 4 · DeepSeek apologises for ~5-hour outage, refunds 7 days of API spend as credits

`I = 67.9` · Products & agents · China · DeepSeek

> 原标题：DeepSeek致歉服务故障并补偿：7日内API消耗金额将全额返还

DeepSeek's open platform said its service system failed from 08:11 to 13:04 Beijing time on Oct 15, making the API unusable. ### 5 · NYC Comptroller Levine urges Anthropic to pause IPO over AI safety and governance

`I = 64.3` · Policy & safety · US · Anthropic, NYC Comptroller Mark Levine

New York City Comptroller Mark Levine sent Anthropic a letter dated Oct 14 asking it to put AI safety and governance ahead of going public, arguing IPO preparation diverts resources from safety work. The letter cites Dario Amodei's Sept 12 essay calling for slower capability development and the draft S-1's reported catastrophic-risk warning, and asks for a written response with a timeline for audits and controls. The comptroller's office has no direct power over the offering.

**Why it matters.** A major public-pension steward publicly pressing a pause adds institutional pressure just as the largest-ever IPO is being marketed.

**Key numbers.** letter dated Oct 14 · IPO valuation up to $2T
