### 1 · China proposes adding 40 AI, biotech and solar technologies to its export-control catalogue

`I = 80.9` · Policy & safety · China · MOFCOM, Ministry of Science and Technology

> 原标题：商务部、科技部就修订《中国禁止出口限制出口技术目录》公开征求意见并对相关技术出口实施临时登记管理

MOFCOM and the Ministry of Science and Technology (Announcement No. 48) opened consultation on revising the Catalogue of Technologies Prohibited and Restricted from Export, adding 40 technologies: 23 in AI (large models, agents, compute clusters, information processing), 12 in photovoltaics and 5 in biomedicine. Xinhua reports 14 would be banned outright and 26 restricted. Pre-export temporary registration applies from 16 Oct. A ministry spokesperson called the AI scope 'precise and limited': technology already public via open source or papers is excluded, and serving models through API, app or SaaS is not treated as technology export.

**Why it matters.** First time model, agent and compute-cluster know-how appears in China's export-control catalogue, which could shape overseas licensing and joint ventures by Chinese AI and solar firms.

**Key numbers.** 40 technologies · 23 AI / 12 solar / 5 biomedicine · 14 prohibited, 26 restricted · Comments 16 Oct to 15 Nov

### 2 · Reflection AI releases Beam, a 501B-parameter Apache 2.0 open-weight MoE for agentic coding

`I = 69.0` · Models & labs · US · Reflection AI, Amazon Web Services, Hugging Face

Reflection AI released Beam, a mixture-of-experts model with 501B total and about 23B active parameters and a 1M-token context window. Weights are on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0 in BF16, FP8 and NVFP4, and AWS published a launch post for running it. Reflection self-reports 80.9 on SWE-bench Verified and 77.2 on SWE-bench Pro v2-Hard, behind Kimi K3 (88.2) and GLM 5.3 (84.3) on the latter. No independent evaluations or per-token prices were found.

**Why it matters.** A large US-built open-weight model with a permissive licence aimed at agentic coding, a segment dominated by Chinese open models. It follows reported Nvidia interest in Reflection.

**Key numbers.** 501B total / ~23B active params · 1M-token context · Apache 2.0 · SWE-bench Verified 80.9 (self-reported) · SWE-bench Pro v2-Hard 77.2

### 4 · Taiwan plans up to $4B Texas industrial park for Nvidia's supply chain, minister tells Bloomberg

`I = 62.1` · Compute & chips · Global · Taiwan Ministry of Economic Affairs, Nvidia, TSMC, Foxconn

Taiwan's economy minister Kung Ming-hsin said the government wants US industrial parks for smaller suppliers to TSMC, Foxconn and other AI-hardware makers, starting with a Texas complex costing as much as $4B. Each site is estimated at $2B-$4B; an industry association is expected to raise funds with government loan guarantees, visas and land access. Nvidia, TSMC and Foxconn have made no commitment and no suppliers or schedule were named.

**Why it matters.** Taiwan is moving to anchor the AI server supply chain inside the US, reinforcing the US-Taiwan trade deal.

**Key numbers.** Up to $4B for the Texas complex · $2B-$4B per site

### 5 · Anthropic: Claude Mythos 5 designs quantum circuits beating published ones, up to 17x fewer gates

`I = 61.6` · Research · US · Anthropic, Harvard, QuEra

In an Anthropic STEM Fellows project with Harvard and QuEra collaborators (arXiv 2610.22367), Claude Mythos 5 improved on the best published circuits on all eight tasks given. A 53-hour autonomous run produced an elliptic-curve point-addition design using 12% more qubits than the smallest prior design but 17x fewer gates; for Decoded Quantum Interferometry it swapped in Berlekamp-Massey decoding, cutting decoder gates about 12x versus Google's prior construction. These are resource-estimate gains, not hardware runs.

**Why it matters.** It points to AI agents finding algorithmic improvements relevant to the cost of attacking elliptic-curve cryptography, though only in circuit estimates.

**Key numbers.** 8 circuit tasks improved · 17x fewer gates (+12% qubits) · ~12x fewer decoder gates · 53-hour autonomous run
