### 1 · Elvis Presley rightsholders sue Anthropic over alleged training on 274 songs

`I = 57.8` · Policy & safety · US · Anthropic, Authentic Brands Group

A subsidiary of Authentic Brands Group, with other Presley rights owners, sued Anthropic on Oct 23 in the US District Court for the Northern District of California. The complaint alleges Claude was trained on 274 songs without a licence and can still produce infringing derivative versions when asked to update songs for a modern audience. Reports put statutory damages at up to $150,000 per song. Counsel is Richard Busch, who won the Marvin Gaye estate's 'Blurred Lines' case. Anthropic had not commented in the coverage seen.

**Why it matters.** It is another music-copyright suit against Anthropic, on top of the publishers' case, and it lands as the company heads toward an IPO where litigation exposure must be disclosed.

**Key numbers.** 274 songs · Up to $150,000 per song · Filed Oct 23, 2026

### 2 · Musk says SpaceX closed $40B of debt for GPUs, predicts neocloud shakeout

`I = 53.2` · Business & funding · US · SpaceX, Apollo, Nvidia

On Oct 25 Elon Musk posted on X that SpaceX had closed $40B of debt to buy GPUs, mostly callable debt for chips arriving in Q1 and Q2, and 'not a major factor this quarter'. He credited SpaceX's investment-grade status and Starlink and launch revenue. He also said debt markets have nearly shut off financing for firms without substantial revenue outside AI data centres, and that more than 200 neoclouds face consolidation. Earlier reports described an Apollo-led package of bank loans and investment-grade debt tied to an Nvidia chip purchase.

**Why it matters.** Capital for GPU purchases is concentrating in large rated borrowers while neocloud credit tightens, a theme running through this month's neocloud selloff.

**Key numbers.** $40B debt · GPUs arriving Q1-Q2 2027 · 200+ neoclouds said to face consolidation

*Note: Search snippets only. The close rests on Musk's own X post; no statement from SpaceX, Apollo or the banks was seen. An earlier FT report said the deal would close in 2027, which conflicts. SpaceX listing status/ticker unconfirmed, so no ticker is tagged.*

### 3 · Moonshot says Kimi K3.1 Pro had cyber-offense data removed in training; internal exploit benchmark falls from 90.5% to 81.0%

`I = 52.8` · Models & labs · China · Moonshot AI

> 原标题：中国最强开源模型杀到！Kimi K3.1 Pro剥离网络攻击数据：编程仍反超GPT-6

Moonshot AI says Kimi K3.1 Pro, a post-training upgrade of K3, had cyber-offense and defense data identified and removed during mid-training and reinforcement learning. Chinese media report an internal benchmark, xdev-bench-hard, scoring 90.5% for the unfiltered variant versus 81.0% for the released model. The company reportedly says it will not open-weight a model at the absolute cyber frontier, and full weights are due by Nov 4. Reports also cite a 1M-token context and Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 65.7%.

**Why it matters.** It is a rare public case of a Chinese open-weight lab describing data filtering to cap offensive cyber capability.

**Key numbers.** xdev-bench-hard 90.5% unfiltered vs 81.0% released · Terminal-Bench 4.0: 65.7% · 1M-token context · Weights due by Nov 4
