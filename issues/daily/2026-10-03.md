### 2 · Huawei's Xu Zhijun says Ascend has overtaken Nvidia in China; supply still short of demand

`I = 64.3` · Compute & chips · China · Huawei, Nvidia, Bernstein Research · `NVDA`

> 原标题：华为徐直军确认昇腾中国市场份额已超英伟达

Huawei rotating chairman Eric Xu said Ascend AI accelerators have surpassed Nvidia in China by sales and market share. Chinese outlets paired the remark with Bernstein-style figures of roughly 50% for Ascend versus about 8% for Nvidia, against 3% vs 95% in 2022. Xu said Huawei lacks capacity for large-scale overseas expansion and expects AI model training to move onto Ascend 950DT SuperPoD/SuperCluster systems from next year; he put global compute supply-demand balance around 2029 and China's around 2030.

**Why it matters.** A senior Huawei executive publicly claiming market leadership marks the clearest sign yet of domestic substitution in China's AI compute, while the capacity caveat shows supply, not demand, is the constraint.

**Key numbers.** ~50% Ascend China AI-chip share (Bernstein figure per reports) · ~8% Nvidia China share vs 95% in 2022 · Ascend 950DT SuperPoD training from 2027 · Supply-demand balance: global ~2029, China ~2030

### 3 · Aleph Alpha releases Kolibri, a 78B-parameter open-weight German MoE under Apache 2.0

`I = 63.7` · Models & labs · Europe · Aleph Alpha, Hugging Face

Aleph Alpha released Kolibri, a sovereign open-weight mixture-of-experts model for German and English, with 78.1B total and about 3.46B active parameters per token. It has a native 262,144-token context, validated to 1M tokens via hybrid attention, and weights are on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0. Search coverage says it was trained on about 24T tokens (21.3% German) on infrastructure in Germany and Finland, with a custom tokenizer cutting token counts 11-15% versus standard ones.

**Why it matters.** A credible European open-weight model aimed at public-sector sovereignty requirements, with very low active-parameter cost for self-hosting.

**Key numbers.** 78.1B total params · ~3.46B active per token · 262,144-token native context (1M validated) · ~24T training tokens · Apache 2.0
