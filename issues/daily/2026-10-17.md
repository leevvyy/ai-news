### 2 · Anthropic privately pitches Congress a federal frontier AI safety bill, Politico reports

`I = 70.5` · Policy & safety · US · Anthropic, US Congress, US Department of Commerce

Politico reported on Oct 16 that Anthropic circulated a confidential draft federal frontier-AI safety bill to members of Congress, and Bloomberg and Reuters followed. Per secondhand accounts, the government could seek to halt a model only for a foreseeable, imminent catastrophic threat (over $1B in damages or more than 50 people killed or seriously injured, tied to bioweapons, loss of control or cyberattacks), with judicial approval required and only Commerce able to seek the order. Independent evaluators would register with Commerce and emergent risks would be reported within 72 hours. Anthropic says it reflects its Advanced AI Framework.

**Why it matters.** A leading lab drafting the federal rules for halting frontier models shapes the preemption debate before the midterms.

**Key numbers.** $1B damages threshold · 50+ people killed or seriously injured · 72-hour risk reporting · Commerce Dept sole authority to seek a halt order

*Note: Politico original URL not seen; bill text unpublished, details are secondhand from Politico via search snippets. Coverage count from harvest cluster (Bloomberg, Reuters, Politico, Mother Jones etc.).*

### 3 · Taiwan to make smuggling restricted AI servers and chips to China a crime, minister tells Bloomberg

`I = 59.9` · Policy & safety · Other · Taiwan Ministry of Economic Affairs, Nvidia · `NVDA`

Taiwan's economy minister told Bloomberg that AI servers and advanced semiconductors will be added to a controlled-items list, after which shipping restricted AI hardware to China becomes a criminal offence under the Foreign Trade Act. Reports link the move to commitments in the 2026 US-Taiwan Agreement on Reciprocal Trade, say penalties and start date are undecided, and note a DPP lawmaker has drafted a mainland-China chip clause. Prosecutors have so far relied on forgery and breach-of-trust laws, including an August indictment of nine people over 74 Nvidia B300 servers.

**Why it matters.** Closes a legal gap in a key chip-diversion route and aligns Taipei with US export controls.

**Key numbers.** Foreign Trade Act amendment · 74 Nvidia B300 servers in August indictment · nine people indicted

*Note: Bloomberg original dated Oct 16 (time not seen, possibly before window); harvest cluster Oct 17. Minister name differs across outlets (Kung Ming-hsin in most, Kuo Jyh-huei in one), omitted. Details from search snippets. Nvidia is not a party, only the chips involved; ticker included as the named chip subject.*

### 4 · Anthropic offers Blackstone over 10% yield in roughly $60B Google TPU chip financing, FT reports

`I = 59.6` · Business & funding · US · Anthropic, Blackstone, Broadcom, Google · `BX` `AVGO`

The FT, relayed by Reuters on Oct 16, reports Anthropic is offering Blackstone a double-digit yield on its latest chip-financing deal, called Project North Star. Blackstone funds would take about $9B of an $18B junior tranche backed by Anthropic's five-year chip lease payments, with $42B of Broadcom-backed senior debt, funding roughly 1.5GW of Google TPUs. Similarly rated borrowers reportedly pay about 7% in public markets; the deal has not closed and Blackstone declined to comment.

**Why it matters.** The price Anthropic pays for compute debt is a live signal on AI capex financing stress amid the semiconductor sell-off.

**Key numbers.** >10% yield · $9B Blackstone junior debt · $18B junior tranche · $42B senior debt (Broadcom-backed) · ~1.5GW Google TPUs
