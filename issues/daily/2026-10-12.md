### 1 · Amazon in talks to buy AI-compute startup Decart for about $7B, WSJ reports

`I = 61.7` · Business & funding · US · Amazon, Decart, Anthropic · `AMZN`

The Wall Street Journal reports Amazon is negotiating to acquire Decart, an AI startup whose software runs AI workloads across Nvidia GPUs, Google TPUs and Amazon's Trainium chips, in a deal valued at about $7 billion. The deal could close soon if talks hold, but another bidder could still emerge. Anthropic had been in talks at about $6B but walked away after due diligence in September.

**Why it matters.** A $7B purchase would be a large bid by AWS for cross-chip inference software, aimed at easing Trainium adoption against Nvidia's software lock-in.

**Key numbers.** ~$7B reported price · ~$4B valuation in May (PitchBook, per WSJ) · Anthropic ~$6B talks abandoned in Sept
