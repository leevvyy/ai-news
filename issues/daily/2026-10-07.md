### 1 · SpaceX seeks about $40B of debt, led by Apollo, to buy Nvidia chips, FT reports

`I = 66.1` · Compute & chips · US · SpaceX, Nvidia, Apollo, PIMCO

### 2 · Marvell lifts FY2028 revenue target to about $20B and sets FY2031 range of $70-90B at investor day

`I = 61.3` · Compute & chips · US · Marvell, Google, Broadcom · `MRVL`

At its Oct 6 investor day in New York, Marvell raised its fiscal 2028 revenue target from $18B to about $20B, above the $18.2B consensus cited by coverage, and set a first fiscal 2031 range of $70-90B. It lifted its FY2029 custom-silicon target to above $12B and cited a $400B AI addressable market by 2030. Coverage ties the targets to an August Google agreement with warrants linked to up to $120B of cumulative custom-chip orders; the stock rose over 7% intraday.

**Why it matters.** It reads as a bet that hyperscaler custom ASIC and optical interconnect demand will outgrow merchant GPUs, a direct challenge to Broadcom, with a hyperscaler-warrant structure that ties customer orders to Marvell equity.

### 3 · Anthropic launches Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens for prompts under 100K, matching GPT-6 Luna

`I = 57.2` · Models & labs · US · Anthropic, OpenAI

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, its small model, with API pricing of $0.10 input and $0.50 output per million tokens for prompts up to 100,000 tokens (down from $1/$5 on Haiku 4.5) and $0.50/$2.50 above that. Anthropic reports 72.4% on OSWorld 2.1 (offline subset; 15.7% for Haiku 4.5) and 39.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (0% for Haiku 4.5), and puts average savings near 75% after a tokenizer change that uses slightly more tokens per task. The model also adds adjustable effort levels.

**Why it matters.** The base rate matches OpenAI's smallest model, GPT-6 Luna, so the small-model tier is now a price fight. Cheap computer-use and agentic-coding capability at this level lowers the cost of running sub-agent fleets, though the 100K-token step-up and tokenizer change blunt the headline cut for long-context agent loops.

**Key numbers.** $0.10/$0.50 per M tokens (<=100K prompts) · $0.50/$2.50 per M tokens (>100K) · ~75% average cost saving (Anthropic) · 72.4% OSWorld 2.1 (offline subset) · 39.2% Terminal-Bench 4.0

### 5 · Google DeepMind releases EmbeddingGemma 2, an open multimodal embedding model for on-device search

`I = 49.6` · Models & labs · US · Google DeepMind, Google · `GOOGL`

Google DeepMind published EmbeddingGemma 2, an open-weight embedding model that maps text, images, video and audio into one shared vector space for search and organisation on local devices. One report puts it at about 740 million parameters. It was first posted on October 6.

**Why it matters.** An open, small multimodal embedder lets developers build private on-device retrieval without calling a hosted API such as Gemini Embedding 2. It extends the Gemma line into retrieval, a core piece of agent memory stacks.

**Key numbers.** ~740M parameters (single report)
