### 1 · OpenAI tells investors run rate is ~$50B, ~$20B below prior figure; Nvidia, Oracle, CoreWeave fall

`I = 72.2` · Business & funding · US · OpenAI, Nvidia, Oracle, CoreWeave · `NVDA` `ORCL` `CRWV`

The FT and CNBC reported OpenAI told investors in a presentation that annualized revenue was about $50B at end of September, versus the ~$68B widely reported late last month. **Why it matters.** It is the first hard test of whether the AI-capex chain (chips, Oracle, neoclouds) can lean on OpenAI revenue ahead of its planned 2027 IPO. Markets treated an accounting gap as a demand signal.

**Key numbers.** ~$50B run rate vs ~$68B prior · $70B+ year-end target · NVDA -2.94% to $230.48 · Nasdaq -1.25%

### 2 · Nvidia-backed Firmus shelves roughly $5B ASX IPO on weak demand, will raise privately

`I = 60.7` · Compute & chips · Other · Firmus, Nvidia · `NVDA`

Reuters reported that Firmus, the Australian AI data-centre operator backed by Nvidia, shelved its IPO, citing market volatility and saying the terms did not reflect its business. It had planned A$11 shares, implying an equity valuation of US$30.6B, nearly triple the ~$10.5B of its early-August round. Reuters said investors balked at only two operating data centres and no build track record, with analysts estimating debt near $30B. Bloomberg framed the pulled deal as a warning for AI funding.

**Why it matters.** A flagship Nvidia-ecosystem neocloud failing to price its listing is a concrete sign of investor fatigue on AI infrastructure valuations and leverage.

**Key numbers.** ~US$5B IPO shelved · A$11 per share · US$30.6B implied equity value · ~$10.5B August round valuation · ~$30B debt (analyst est.)

*Note: Reuters details come from search snippets; some syndicates are dated Oct 8 US time. Offering size variously reported as US$5B.*

### 3 · Nvidia plans to invest in inference-chip rival d-Matrix, The Information reports

`I = 55.8` · Compute & chips · US · Nvidia, d-Matrix · `NVDA`

The Information reported that Nvidia plans to invest in d-Matrix, a seven-year-old AI inference chip developer, citing three people with knowledge. d-Matrix is to connect its next-gen Raptor XPUs to Nvidia's platform via NVLink Fusion. d-Matrix last raised $275M at a $2B valuation in Nov 2025 and has been seeking at least $5B. Neither company has confirmed, and terms are unclear.

**Why it matters.** Nvidia is absorbing would-be challengers into its ecosystem, mirroring its $20B Groq licensing deal in December.

**Key numbers.** $275M last round (Nov 2025) · $2B last valuation · $5B+ valuation sought · $20B Groq licensing deal (Dec)

### 4 · USA Today Co. and affiliated papers sue OpenAI in SDNY, seeking over $250M

`I = 52.4` · Policy & safety · US · USA Today Co., OpenAI

USA Today Co. (formerly Gannett) and affiliated newspapers sued OpenAI in Manhattan federal court on Oct 8, alleging GPT models were trained on hundreds of thousands of their copyrighted articles and that outputs substitute for the originals. The complaint also alleges tools stripped copyright management information. It seeks damages above $250M, an injunction and destruction of models and training sets built on the material. Plaintiff count is reported as 13 or 19 publications depending on the outlet.

**Why it matters.** Another large publisher group joins the copyright fight against OpenAI, adding damages and model-destruction exposure just as OpenAI prepares for an IPO.

**Key numbers.** >$250M damages sought · 13 or 19 plaintiffs (reports differ)

*Note: Plaintiff list is snippet-only and differs across outlets. Published about 4h before window start; still developing.*
